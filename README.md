# 📚 Reading-IELTS — Thematic Academic Reading Vault

A curated vault of authentic IELTS Academic Reading passages categorized into 4 core high-frequency themes: **Environment**, **History**, **People**, and **Science**. Features focused comprehension drills, timed practice, and lexical glossaries.

---

## 🌟 4 Thematic Categories

1. **🌿 Environment**: Climate change, wildlife conservation, renewable energy, rainforest ecology, marine ecosystems.
2. **🏛️ History**: Ancient civilizations, archeological discoveries, industrial revolution, evolutionary history.
3. **👥 People & Society**: Psychology, cognitive science, sociology, language evolution, education systems.
4. **🔬 Science & Technology**: Astronomy, medical innovations, artificial intelligence, genetics, material physics.
